What We Do

Our Services

Professional arboriculture and garden maintenance for residential and commercial properties across Melbourne's south east.

Tree Removal

Safe and efficient removal of trees of all sizes. Whether it's a dead tree, storm-damaged or just in the wrong spot, we'll take care of it.

  • Full tree removal & stump grinding
  • Dead or hazardous tree removal
  • Complete site cleanup

Hedge Trimming

Keep your hedges looking sharp and well-maintained all year round. We handle everything from small garden hedges to large boundary screens.

  • Formal & informal hedge shaping
  • Boundary & privacy screens
  • Regular maintenance programs

Yard Cleanups

Overgrown backyard? Moving in or out? We'll get your yard looking its best with a thorough cleanup from top to bottom.

  • Green waste removal
  • Overgrown garden restoration
  • End-of-lease yard tidy

Gutter Cleaning

Blocked gutters cause real damage. We clear out leaves, debris and buildup to keep your gutters flowing and your home protected.

  • Full gutter & downpipe clearing
  • Leaf & debris removal
  • Seasonal maintenance

Storm Damage & Reactive Works

When storms hit, you need someone fast. We respond quickly to clear fallen trees, broken branches and debris so your property is safe again.

  • Emergency fallen tree removal
  • Broken branch clearing
  • Post-storm site cleanup
